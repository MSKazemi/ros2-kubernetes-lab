Phase 4 · days 61–90

Grow with evidence, not with hype

Once the lab has something verified to show, this phase shares it, listens to what real people run into, and fixes the friction they report. Growth here means more people reaching a working first run, not a bigger number on a badge.

Proposal · planning targets, not promisesIssues #21, #22Source: docs/ROADMAP-2026.md

A loop, not a launch

Publishing is where the loop starts, not where it ends. Each lap uses what people actually report, and the roadmap plans to repeat it every quarter.

Stars are an imperfect signal

A star does not show that anyone ran the lab. The roadmap treats successful first runs, outside contributors, documented reuse and reproducible results as better signs, and says to measure feedback quality too. Stars are never incentivised.

How to help

  • Pick a small task and comment that you would like to work on it.
  • When a supported quickstart exists, try it and report exactly where it failed or confused you.
  • If you teach ROS 2 or Kubernetes, review a tutorial and tell us what a student would stumble on.
  • Fixing a broken link or an unclear sentence counts as a real contribution.
